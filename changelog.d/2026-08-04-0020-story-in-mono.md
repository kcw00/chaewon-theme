## 2026-08-04 00:20 — The story runs in mono

Branch: `update`.

### Context
`01 The story` was Inter, the same face as the rest of the site's prose.
Requested in the same face as the chips under `03 Built with` — that is
`mono`, JetBrains Mono.

### Changed
**`style.css`** — `.project__body > p` takes
`--wp--preset--font-family--mono` at `0.9375rem` with `line-height: 1.8`.

Both adjustments are load-bearing. A monospace at the body's 17px reads
optically larger than Inter and, at Inter's 1.7, lands as a code block
rather than prose. Stepping the size down and opening the leading is
what keeps it readable at paragraph length.

`max-width` stays `60ch`. `ch` is measured in the element's own font, so
the measure is still 60 characters — it just occupies more physical
width in a monospace.

### Verification
`.project__body > p` matches the story paragraphs and nothing else: the
chapter rules nest their text inside `.chapter-rule`, `02` is a `ul`,
and `03` is `.stack-group` divs. On AI Telecom the selector returns
exactly the three story paragraphs.

```
                 family            size    line-height
story            JetBrains Mono    15px    27px
02 bullets       Inter             17px    28.9px
03 chips         JetBrains Mono    11px    16.5px

story face === chip face   true
measure                    60 characters
```

390px: no horizontal overflow, story 366px in a 390px viewport. Dark
scheme checked on Minion. No console errors. One rule, so all ten
project pages carry it.

### Rollback
`git revert` this commit. No content change — CSS only.

### For LLMs
- The selector is `> p`, a direct child. The chips under `03` are also
  `p` elements, so a descendant selector would restyle those too and
  fight `.stack-group__items p` on specificity.
- **`02 What it does` is still Inter.** The story and the bullets are
  both prose and no longer share a face. That was the literal scope of
  the request; if the two should match, the same declarations go on
  `.project__body > ul, .project__body > ol`.
- Size and leading are not incidental. Dropping either one back to the
  Inter values makes the paragraph read as code.
